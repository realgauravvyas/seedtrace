# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

We strive to keep seedtrace a welcoming, rigorous, and harassment-free
community. Be kind, assume good faith, critique code not people, and
remember that everyone here got bitten by nondeterminism at some point.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (issues,
discussions, PRs, releases) and in public spaces when an individual
represents the project.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may
be reported to the maintainer via the contact info in
[`pyproject.toml`](pyproject.toml) or through GitHub's reporting tools.
All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
